chronark

Fund your account

Your Chronark account is the key on your machine. Pay into it from any wallet and the node credits it once the deposit is confirmed.

Deposits open as soon as the vault contract is live. Until then a node operator can credit your account directly.

  1. Get your account id: chronark account
  2. Deposit here. Your wallet asks twice: approve the amount, then deposit.
  3. Check it landed: chronark account --cloud <node>

Deposits pay for compute on this node and are not withdrawable back to your wallet. Unused session budget always returns to your balance.
